## Direction contract

THESIS: The expert's words are a galley and model elements are keyed proof marks. The signature interaction is selecting either a sentence or its mark and seeing the other highlighted.

OWN-WORLD: White stock, dark ink, correction red, hairline rules, readable book text, plain sans controls, numbered lines and compact passed stamps. Flat sheets with no decorative imagery.

STORY: Describe a process, inspect its extracted meaning, correct misunderstandings, pass each gate, and test the resulting plan against changing facts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim masthead with the run name and rack access; a narrow routing slip at left; the numbered description occupying the largest centre-left column; model annotations aligned in the right margin. The current gate sits at the bottom of the working sheet. Selection, findings, and an inline editor share the annotation region.
